About the Company

CheckMia Technologies is a student-led technology startup concept focused on helping consumers assess suspicious footwear listings. The proposed platform combines product identifiers, reference records and computer-vision signals to provide an understandable assessment.

Industry

Vision, Mission & Objectives

Vision

A more transparent digital shopping environment where buyers can make informed decisions.

Mission

Explore accessible AI-assisted product checks while communicating uncertainty and encouraging trusted verification.

Objectives

  • Build a footwear-focused prototype.
  • Evaluate on a permissioned, labelled dataset.

Proposed Project: CheckMia Verify

Counterfeit footwear can mislead buyers, create financial loss and weaken trust in online marketplaces. CheckMia proposes a decision-support prototype that combines image classification, identifier checks and transparent confidence reporting.

  1. Enter brand, model and product code.
  2. Upload front, side, heel and label images.
  3. System checks data quality and runs a baseline image model.
  4. Results show matching signals, uncertainty and next steps.

Safety boundary: The prototype will not certify a product as genuine. It will label results as “consistent with reference”, “suspicious indicators” or “unable to assess”.
